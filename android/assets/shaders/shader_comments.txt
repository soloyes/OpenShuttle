Vertex.shader

When SpriteBatch passes a colour to the shader, the ABGR int colour is converted to a float.
To avoid NaN in that conversion, alpha can't use the full range, only 0-254.
So to pass a fully opaque colour (alpha 1.0 as a float), the alpha has to be scaled up.
This is a libGDX quirk; keep it in mind when you override the vertex shader.
